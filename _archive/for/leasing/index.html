For leasing teams

Focus human judgment where it changes the renter decision.

Give leasing teams the context, priorities, and next-best actions needed to move qualified renters toward the right home.

The leasing mandateConvert intent without losing the person inside the process.

Use intelligence to clarify attention and timing—not to automate every interaction indiscriminately.

What changesThe queue becomes a decision system.

Leaser connects prospect behavior, inventory fit, operating urgency, and prior interactions so leasing teams can understand who needs attention, why the moment matters, and what action is appropriate.

  • Prioritize prospects by intent, fit, urgency, and recoverability
  • Preserve context across channels and team handoffs
  • Coordinate follow-up with real inventory and property conditions
  • Escalate consequential or sensitive decisions to people
  • Connect the action to tour, application, approval, and lease outcomes

Decisions Leaser supports

Turn operating context into better timing and attention.

  1. IntentWho is ready to move?

    Recognize meaningful progress without reducing a renter to a lead score.

  2. FitWhich inventory can satisfy the decision?

    Keep unit, floorplan, timing, and preference context connected.

  3. ActionWhat should happen next?

    Recommend the useful next step within clear policy and authority.

  4. OutcomeDid the intervention help?

    Return conversion evidence so the system and team learn together.
