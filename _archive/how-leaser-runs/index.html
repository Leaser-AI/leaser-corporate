How Leaser Runs

Intelligence the enterprise can inspect, govern, and trust.

Leaser operates as a decision system: grounded in evidence, bounded by authority, connected to execution, and accountable to measurable outcomes.

The operating contractEvery action should explain why it happened.

And every outcome should change what the system is prepared to do next.

The operating sequence

From observation to accountable learning.

  1. GroundResolve the current operating state.

    Use shared identity, definitions, timestamps, lineage, and evidence classes.

  2. ProposeMake the decision explicit.

    State the objective, expected effect, rationale, confidence, and alternatives.

  3. GovernApply the right authority boundary.

    Observe, recommend, request approval, or act within a bounded policy.

  4. ExecuteCarry the decision into the operating system.

    Preserve idempotency, status, failure handling, and an action receipt.

  5. MeasureCompare outcome with expectation.

    Use the strongest practical counterfactual and declare uncertainty.

  6. RenewExpand, narrow, or revoke authority.

    Let measured performance determine what Leaser may do next.

Governed autonomy

Authority belongs to a decision class—not to AI in general.

ObserveWatch and learn

No action authority.

RecommendPropose with evidence

A person decides.

ApproveAct after consent

Execution follows explicit approval.

ExecuteOperate within bounds

Authority is narrow, measurable, and renewable.

Decision receipt

The record travels with the action.

StateWhat was true?

Evidence and operating context at decision time.

IntentWhat should change?

Target outcome, expected effect, and measurement window.

AuthorityWhy could Leaser act?

Policy, approver, limits, and stop conditions.

ExecutionWhat actually happened?

Action status, integration response, failures, and recovery.

OutcomeWhat did the decision change?

Actual effect, counterfactual, confidence, and resulting memory.
