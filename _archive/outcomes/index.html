Outcomes

Proof should follow the decision all the way to the asset.

Leaser measures success through the economic outcomes the portfolio exists to produce—not the activity a channel happens to report.

One proof destinationFrom demand economics to portfolio value.

Client evidence and validated outcome claims will live here as the proof base develops.

The outcome spine

Every layer must connect to the layer below it.

Demand economicsWas demand created where it had marginal value?

Incremental reach, qualified demand, displacement risk, and acquisition economics.

ConversionDid more qualified renters progress?

Response, tour, application, approval, and lease progression by context.

Leasing performanceDid operating velocity improve?

Exposure, days vacant, lease velocity, and performance against plan.

Revenue + occupancyDid the asset outcome change?

Signed leases, achieved rent, profitable occupancy, revenue, and NOI contribution.

Decision performanceDid the intervention work because we acted?

Expected versus actual effect, counterfactual, confidence, and learning returned.

Evidence discipline

A metric is not proof until its origin and limits are visible.

Observed

What happened?

Preserve the operating result without overstating why it occurred.

Estimated

What would likely have happened?

Construct the comparison needed to understand incrementality.

Learned

What should change next?

Return the evidence, confidence, and conditions to portfolio memory.

Client outcomes will be published with the decision, measurement method, and evidentiary boundary intact.
