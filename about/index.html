About Leaser

A new category. Built to change the economics of multifamily.

Leaser introduces whole-asset decision intelligence: one AI-native platform that continuously learns, evaluates alternatives, and coordinates action across demand, leasing, and revenue. Built to change how teams work, how properties perform, and how owners create value.

Built for higher NOI, stronger asset performance, and greater enterprise value.

01 / Why we exist

One asset. Too many separate decisions.

Property performance is shaped by decisions across marketing, leasing, revenue, operations, and ownership. Too often, those decisions are made in separate systems, with different information and different definitions of success.

A campaign can look efficient while the cost of securing a lease rises. A concession can increase lease signings while reducing rental income. Today’s occupancy can look healthy while next month’s vacancies build.

We created Leaser around a simple belief: decisions about the same asset should work toward the same result.

Marketing sees

A cheaper lead.

Ownership needs

A more profitable lease.

Leasing sees

Another signed lease.

Ownership needs

Income after the incentive.

A report shows

Occupancy today.

The team needs

Time to prevent the next vacancy.

Our standard

Don’t manage the work.Change the outcome.

A completed task matters when it helps the property perform. We hold our work to the effect it has on income, cost, the renter experience, and the people responsible for the result.

02 / What we’re building

The whole asset. In every decision.

Leaser brings market conditions, demand, digital experience, prospect engagement, leasing, and revenue into one decision system. Media budgets reflect which units need to lease and when. Prospect conversations inform the next campaign. Concessions are evaluated against their effect on occupancy and net rental income.

The system models alternatives, weighs their expected economic effect, and coordinates action within approved limits. It compares what happened with what was expected, carrying that learning into the next decision.

04 / What we believe

Every decision should earn its way to NOI.

01

Protect income. Eliminate waste.

Media spend, listing costs, concessions, and software all draw from the same economics. We evaluate them against the rental income they help produce and the cost of getting there. More activity only matters when the asset benefits.

02

Own the outcome.

A recommendation creates responsibility. Follow it through the decision, the action, and the result. Measure the economic effect, make the reasoning visible, and change course when the evidence calls for it.

03

Let your team take all the credit.

Give teams a clear view of performance, a plan they can act on, and reporting that speaks ownership’s language. They should walk into the room ready to explain what changed, what it means for NOI, and what comes next.
