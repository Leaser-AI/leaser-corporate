Careers at Leaser

Associate Product Designer

Help make powerful technology feel clear, useful, and considered—one interaction at a time.

Product & DesignJunior
Apply for this role

What we’re building.

Leaser is an AI technology company building connected intelligence for multifamily. Our platform brings market, demand, prospect, leasing, and revenue context together to help teams understand the situation, evaluate their options, and coordinate the next move to continuously improve NOI and enterprise asset value.

01

The opportunity

You’ll support the design of experiences that help multifamily teams understand performance and take the next step with confidence. Work with product, engineering, and design partners to turn research and requirements into thoughtful interfaces. Your contribution will be visible in the details: the empty state, the explanation, the form, and the path through a complicated task.

02

What you’ll build

  • Explore interface concepts through sketches, flows, prototypes, and polished screen designs.
  • Help prepare usability sessions and turn observations into specific design improvements.
  • Use and improve shared components, typography, interaction patterns, and accessibility practices.
  • Document expected behavior across loading, error, empty, and successful states.
  • Work with engineers to review implementation and close the gap between intent and experience.
03

What you’ll bring

  • A portfolio that demonstrates clear thinking, visual care, and an understanding of user needs.
  • Comfort with a modern design and prototyping tool such as Figma.
  • Curiosity about complex products and the patience to make them easier to understand.
  • Openness to critique and the ability to explain why you made a design choice.
04

What progress looks like

  • Take a small product experience from exploration through implementation.
  • Help improve a shared component or interaction used across the product.
  • Bring a useful observation from user research into the team’s next decision.

Bring your perspective.

We value curiosity, thoughtful work, and the perspective you bring. Show us how you think, what you care about, and the contribution you want to make.
