Demand Generation Manager

Build demand for a new category and connect the work to qualified opportunities and revenue.

Demand & GrowthMid-level
Apply for this role

What we’re building.

Leaser is an AI technology company building connected intelligence for multifamily. Our platform brings market, demand, prospect, leasing, and revenue context together to help teams understand the situation, evaluate their options, and coordinate the next move to continuously improve NOI and enterprise asset value.

01

The opportunity

Own integrated programs that bring the right multifamily decision-makers into a meaningful conversation with Leaser. You’ll connect audience understanding, clear positioning, creative work, and measurement across the buyer journey. The role combines hands-on campaign execution with the judgment to know when to expand an idea, change it, or stop spending on it.

02

What you’ll do

  • Plan and execute demand programs across paid, owned, partner, and event channels.
  • Build audience and account approaches around real customer needs and buying context.
  • Work with creative, product, and sales partners on offers, messages, and conversion paths.
  • Measure qualified opportunities, pipeline contribution, and acquisition efficiency.
  • Run disciplined experiments and use the results to improve the next allocation of time and spend.
03

What you’ll bring

  • Experience running demand generation for software or another considered enterprise purchase.
  • Hands-on understanding of campaign platforms, CRM data, and conversion measurement.
  • The ability to interpret results without confusing activity with business progress.
  • Strong collaboration and a clear point of view on audience, message, and distribution.
04

What progress looks like

  • Establish a dependable view of which programs create qualified demand.
  • Deliver an integrated campaign with useful learning across the buyer journey.
  • Improve the connection between marketing activity and sales conversations.

Bring your perspective.

We value curiosity, thoughtful work, and the perspective you bring. Show us how you think, what you care about, and the contribution you want to make.
