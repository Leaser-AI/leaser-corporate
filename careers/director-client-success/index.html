Director of Client Success

Build the customer relationships and operating discipline that turn adoption into lasting value.

Client Success & OperationsSenior
Apply for this role

What we’re building.

Leaser is an AI technology company building connected intelligence for multifamily. Our platform brings market, demand, prospect, leasing, and revenue context together to help teams understand the situation, evaluate their options, and coordinate the next move to continuously improve NOI and enterprise asset value.

01

The opportunity

Help shape how Leaser supports customers as portfolios, product capabilities, and the team grow. You’ll connect executive priorities to product adoption and measurable progress, while coaching the people who make those relationships work. The role calls for commercial judgment, operational depth, and an honest view of where the customer is seeing value and where more work is needed.

02

What you’ll do

  • Develop account plans that connect customer goals, adoption, performance reviews, and retention.
  • Lead substantive conversations with portfolio leaders using clear measures and practical recommendations.
  • Coach client success teammates and build repeatable ways to identify risk and follow through.
  • Bring customer evidence into product priorities and help coordinate a response across teams.
  • Improve the journey from implementation through ongoing value realization and renewal.
03

What you’ll bring

  • Experience leading enterprise customer relationships and developing client success talent.
  • Sound judgment in complex accounts, including difficult conversations about expectations and results.
  • An ability to connect adoption and operating measures with the customer’s business goals.
  • Experience in enterprise software, property technology, or multifamily operations.
04

What progress looks like

  • Give strategic accounts a shared view of progress and the decisions that matter next.
  • Establish a useful cadence for adoption, risk, and customer outcomes.
  • Help the team build durable relationships grounded in visible value.

Bring your perspective.

We value curiosity, thoughtful work, and the perspective you bring. Show us how you think, what you care about, and the contribution you want to make.
