Head of Growth

Help a new category find its market. Build the growth strategy with the discipline to prove what works.

Demand & GrowthSenior
Apply for this role

What we’re building.

Leaser is an AI technology company building connected intelligence for multifamily. Our platform brings market, demand, prospect, leasing, and revenue context together to help teams understand the situation, evaluate their options, and coordinate the next move to continuously improve NOI and enterprise asset value.

01

The opportunity

Shape how Leaser reaches customers, explains its value, and builds durable demand. You’ll work across product, marketing, sales, and client success to connect the company’s story with its commercial performance. This is a role for a leader who can move between positioning and execution, grow people as well as programs, and make clear decisions with imperfect information.

02

What you’ll do

  • Set growth priorities around customer segments, market opportunities, and the company’s commercial goals.
  • Connect brand, demand generation, product marketing, and sales enablement into a coherent plan.
  • Build an operating cadence for experiments, resource decisions, and learning.
  • Develop a clear view of acquisition efficiency, conversion, pipeline quality, and customer value.
  • Coach the growth team and partner with leadership on the next stage of the business.
03

What you’ll bring

  • Experience building and leading growth for enterprise software or a similarly complex product.
  • A track record of connecting positioning, distribution, and commercial results.
  • Strong analytical judgment and the ability to distinguish a useful signal from an attractive metric.
  • A willingness to stay close to execution while building systems that help a team scale.
04

What progress looks like

  • Create a shared growth plan with clear choices, owners, and measures.
  • Find repeatable ways to turn the right audience’s interest into qualified demand.
  • Make the next investment decision more informed than the last.

Bring your perspective.

We value curiosity, thoughtful work, and the perspective you bring. Show us how you think, what you care about, and the contribution you want to make.
