Implementation Manager

Turn the decision to use Leaser into a confident launch and a useful new way of working.

Client Success & OperationsMid-level
Apply for this role

What we’re building.

Leaser is an AI technology company building connected intelligence for multifamily. Our platform brings market, demand, prospect, leasing, and revenue context together to help teams understand the situation, evaluate their options, and coordinate the next move to continuously improve NOI and enterprise asset value.

01

The opportunity

Lead implementations from initial scope through readiness, launch, and handoff. You’ll coordinate customer teams with product and engineering, make dependencies visible, and keep the work tied to the customer’s operating priorities. A successful implementation leaves people clear on the data, the permissions, the responsibilities, and the value they should expect to see.

02

What you’ll do

  • Build implementation plans with clear milestones, owners, requirements, and acceptance criteria.
  • Coordinate integration access, data validation, configuration, and launch readiness.
  • Translate customer workflows into setup decisions and useful training.
  • Identify blockers early and bring a practical recommendation to the people who can resolve them.
  • Document the handoff into client success with agreed goals and the next measures of progress.
03

What you’ll bring

  • Experience managing software implementations or complex customer onboarding projects.
  • The ability to work comfortably with both technical and operating teams.
  • A structured approach to dependencies, data quality, risks, and stakeholder communication.
  • An interest in multifamily operations and the judgment to adapt a plan to the customer’s context.
04

What progress looks like

  • Bring a customer through launch with clear ownership and validated readiness.
  • Reduce avoidable delays by improving the implementation process.
  • Give the customer team the confidence to use Leaser in its everyday work.

Bring your perspective.

We value curiosity, thoughtful work, and the perspective you bring. Show us how you think, what you care about, and the contribution you want to make.
