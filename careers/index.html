Our core ethos

The standard is the point.

Twelve principles for how we think, build, decide, and work together.

01

Have a point of view.

“It depends” is occasionally true. It’s rarely useful. Do the work, then take a position.

02

Don’t confuse activity with progress.

Meetings, decks, tickets, and messages aren’t accomplishments. Changed outcomes are.

03

Default to unreasonable.

Most breakthroughs look excessive before they look obvious.

04

Build what should exist.

The category’s current standards are context, not instructions.

05

Make complicated things feel obvious.

Complexity underneath is fine. Complexity for the customer isn’t.

06

Taste is part of the job.

How something looks, sounds, moves and feels matters. Polish isn’t decoration.

07

Kill the workaround.

If we keep doing something manually, awkwardly or repeatedly, fix the system.

08

Question the inherited answer.

“That’s how multifamily works” is an invitation to investigate.

09

Finish the loop.

Don’t stop at insight. Decide. Act. Measure. Learn. Improve.

10

Protect the weird idea.

The idea everyone immediately understands probably already exists.

11

Be suspicious of “best practice.”

It usually means somebody stopped asking why.

12

Revenue is everyone’s business.

Product, engineering, design, client success, and marketing all ultimately touch the customer’s economics.

The operating principle

Own the outcome.Not the assignment.

Your responsibility doesn’t end where the brief does.

Build for the real world.

Connect thoughtful technology to meaningful improvements for the people who use it.
