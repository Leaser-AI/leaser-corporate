Junior Software Engineer

Build useful software. Learn how the whole system works. Help turn complex decisions into better everyday experiences.

Engineering & AIJunior
Apply for this role

What we’re building.

Leaser is an AI technology company building connected intelligence for multifamily. Our platform brings market, demand, prospect, leasing, and revenue context together to help teams understand the situation, evaluate their options, and coordinate the next move to continuously improve NOI and enterprise asset value.

01

The opportunity

You’ll contribute to the product and services behind Leaser’s connected intelligence. This is a role for someone early in their engineering career who enjoys understanding a problem, shipping a thoughtful solution, and learning from what happens in production. You’ll work with experienced engineers and product partners on clearly scoped improvements that real teams use.

02

What you’ll build

  • Build and refine product features, APIs, and internal tools with guidance from the engineering team.
  • Write readable code and meaningful tests; explain your decisions in reviews and documentation.
  • Investigate bugs by tracing the user experience, application behavior, and underlying data.
  • Help connect new interfaces to reliable services and consistent business definitions.
  • Follow a feature through release, feedback, and the next improvement.
03

What you’ll bring

  • Working knowledge of JavaScript or TypeScript, web fundamentals, and version control.
  • Projects, coursework, internships, or professional experience that show how you think and build.
  • An ability to break a problem into smaller steps and ask clear questions when something is unfamiliar.
  • Care for usability, reliability, and the details that make software feel complete.
04

What progress looks like

  • Ship a scoped feature and understand how its behavior is measured.
  • Make a recurring bug or manual task easier for the team to handle.
  • Become a dependable contributor in code review and product discussions.

Bring your perspective.

We value curiosity, thoughtful work, and the perspective you bring. Show us how you think, what you care about, and the contribution you want to make.
