Platform Engineer

Build the dependable foundations that let connected intelligence work in the real world.

Engineering & AIMid-level
Apply for this role

What we’re building.

Leaser is an AI technology company building connected intelligence for multifamily. Our platform brings market, demand, prospect, leasing, and revenue context together to help teams understand the situation, evaluate their options, and coordinate the next move to continuously improve NOI and enterprise asset value.

01

The opportunity

Work across the services, data, and infrastructure behind Leaser. You’ll improve how information enters the system, stays consistent, and supports authorized actions. The role combines hands-on development with practical decisions about reliability and shared architecture.

02

What you’ll build

  • Develop integrations, resilient processing jobs, and dependable APIs.
  • Improve identity matching and traceability across the prospect-to-lease journey.
  • Maintain clear data models and consistent definitions for performance metrics.
  • Strengthen access boundaries, diagnostics, recovery, and deployment practices.
  • Build reusable services for recommendations, approvals, execution, and outcome tracking.
03

What you’ll bring

  • Production experience with TypeScript, Node.js, PostgreSQL, and backend services.
  • Practical understanding of cloud infrastructure, containers, and observability.
  • Experience handling retries, reconciliation, and asynchronous integrations.
  • Judgment about when to simplify a system and when stronger boundaries are necessary.
04

What progress looks like

  • Make a critical workflow more dependable.
  • Improve traceability from source data to business results.
  • Reduce the effort required to diagnose and resolve production issues.

Bring your perspective.

We value curiosity, thoughtful work, and the perspective you bring. Show us how you think, what you care about, and the contribution you want to make.
