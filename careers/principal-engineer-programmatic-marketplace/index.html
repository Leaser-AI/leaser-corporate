Principal Engineer, Programmatic Marketplace

Shape the marketplace that connects demand, intelligent allocation, and measurable leasing outcomes.

Engineering & AISenior
Apply for this role

What we’re building.

Leaser is an AI technology company building connected intelligence for multifamily. Our platform brings market, demand, prospect, leasing, and revenue context together to help teams understand the situation, evaluate their options, and coordinate the next move to continuously improve NOI and enterprise asset value.

01

The opportunity

Help define the engineering foundations of the Leaser Demand Network. You’ll connect marketplace integrations, decision systems, and measurement into a dependable product. This is a senior individual-contributor role for someone who can work through an ambiguous commercial problem, make a clear architectural decision, and stay close to the implementation.

02

What you’ll build

  • Design marketplace integrations and the controls used to coordinate demand across supply partners.
  • Build allocation and pacing services that account for inventory, approved budgets, and leasing outcomes.
  • Establish reliable identity, event, and measurement contracts across the marketplace.
  • Create experimentation paths that distinguish incremental contribution from activity that would have happened anyway.
  • Make marketplace actions observable and recoverable, with clear authorization and operational controls.
  • Set engineering standards, document tradeoffs, and help other engineers make sound decisions.
03

What you’ll bring

  • Experience owning consequential backend, marketplace, or advertising technology systems in production.
  • Strong judgment across API design, event processing, data consistency, and failure recovery.
  • An ability to connect technical decisions to measurement quality and commercial outcomes.
  • Clear communication with engineering, product, and business partners; comfort challenging a weak assumption.
  • Hands-on fluency with a modern backend stack; experience with TypeScript, Node.js, PostgreSQL, or cloud infrastructure is relevant.
04

What progress looks like

  • Establish a clear architecture and deliver a working marketplace integration.
  • Make it possible to trace a demand decision through execution to an observable result.
  • Give the team a repeatable way to test allocation changes and learn from them.

Bring your perspective.

We value curiosity, thoughtful work, and the perspective you bring. Show us how you think, what you care about, and the contribution you want to make.
