Senior Product Designer

Give a new category its interface. Make connected intelligence feel understandable, trustworthy, and worth using.

Product & DesignSenior
Apply for this role

What we’re building.

Leaser is an AI technology company building connected intelligence for multifamily. Our platform brings market, demand, prospect, leasing, and revenue context together to help teams understand the situation, evaluate their options, and coordinate the next move to continuously improve NOI and enterprise asset value.

01

The opportunity

Lead design across complex product experiences that connect signals, decisions, and action. You’ll shape how people understand what Leaser knows, evaluate a recommendation, and remain in control of the next move. This is hands-on design work with room to influence the system, raise the craft, and help a growing team build a coherent product.

02

What you’ll build

  • Lead discovery and design for workflows spanning analysis, simulation, approval, and execution.
  • Make uncertainty, tradeoffs, and system behavior legible in the product experience.
  • Develop prototypes that resolve difficult interaction questions before implementation.
  • Evolve shared design patterns and a visual language that can scale across product areas.
  • Partner closely with engineers through implementation and help designers grow through thoughtful critique.
03

What you’ll bring

  • A strong portfolio of shipped digital products with complex workflows or information design.
  • Evidence of research, systems thinking, interaction design, and careful visual execution.
  • Experience guiding a project through ambiguity and explaining decisions to cross-functional partners.
  • An ability to make sophisticated capability approachable without hiding what users need to understand.
04

What progress looks like

  • Deliver a coherent experience across a consequential product journey.
  • Improve the clarity and usability of a difficult decision or action flow.
  • Leave the design system and the team’s design judgment stronger.

Bring your perspective.

We value curiosity, thoughtful work, and the perspective you bring. Show us how you think, what you care about, and the contribution you want to make.
