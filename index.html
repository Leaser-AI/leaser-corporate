The business outcome

Not more activity.
More valuable occupancy.

Most systems optimize a local metric: more leads, lower cost per lead, faster follow-up, more automated tasks.

Leaser works backward from the asset outcome. It helps determine where profitable demand is needed, what intervention should happen next, and whether that decision created a lease that would not otherwise have occurred.

The intelligence layer

Every outcome makes the next decision stronger.

  1. ObserveSee the real operating state.

    Unify demand, inventory, prospect behavior, lease outcomes, and property economics.

  2. DecideChoose what should happen next.

    Estimate which intervention can create the most incremental value under current conditions.

  3. GovernMatch authority to consequence.

    Recommend, request approval, or act within explicit limits and stop conditions.

  4. MeasureLearn what changed because we acted.

    Compare the result with a credible counterfactual—not merely the last recorded touch.

  5. TransferMake the portfolio smarter.

    Reuse lessons only where context, evidence, and risk justify the transfer.

Built for owner-operators and equity partners

Operate the portfolio as a learning system.

Leaser gives owner-operators and equity partners a decision layer across properties, demand channels, operating teams, and systems of record. It connects what happened at the asset to what should happen next across the portfolio.

  • Allocate demand by inventory pressure and marginal lease value
  • Preserve governance across teams, systems, and decision classes
  • Distinguish incremental outcomes from attributed activity
  • Turn local operating evidence into portfolio memory
