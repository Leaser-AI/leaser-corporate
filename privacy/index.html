This notice covers Leaser’s public website and related communications. Customer platform data is addressed separately, as explained below.

01

Who we are and what this covers

Leaser AI (“Leaser,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) provides this Privacy Policy for leaserai.com and the website forms, subscriptions, and recruiting activities that link to it. This policy is effective October 6, 2026.

This policy describes the personal information associated with those activities, why it is used, how it may be disclosed, and the choices available to you. Personal information is information that identifies you or can reasonably be linked to you.

When Leaser processes prospect, resident, property, or portfolio data on a customer’s behalf through the platform, the applicable customer agreement, data-processing terms, and customer privacy notices govern that processing. This website notice does not replace them. If your request concerns a property’s records, contact the property owner or manager; Leaser can help identify the appropriate route when possible.

02

Information you provide

The information depends on how you interact with us:

Inquiries and demos
Your name, work email, company, optional portfolio information, and the content of your message. If you contact us by phone, you may also provide your phone number and other details needed to respond.
Research and Learn updates
Your email address, the updates you request, and subscription or unsubscribe preferences.
Job applications
Your name, email address, résumé, the role you select, your responses to application questions, and any optional location, professional profile, or portfolio link you submit.
Follow-up communications
Information you choose to provide when corresponding with our team about an inquiry, subscription, or application.

Please provide only information relevant to your request. Do not include government identification numbers, financial account credentials, medical records, or confidential resident information in general website forms. If a later process requires sensitive information, we will identify an appropriate collection method and provide any required notice.

03

Website and device information

Website hosting and embedded form services can generate technical information such as IP address, browser and device type, operating system, pages requested, referring pages, timestamps, and diagnostic or security logs. The information available to Leaser depends on the service and its configuration.

Information about cookies, tracking, and browser signals appears in the next section.

04

Cookies, tracking, and browser signals

The public site uses Tally forms for contact, subscriptions, and applications, and loads fonts and other resources from third-party services. Those services may use cookies or similar technologies under their own notices. Browser settings may let you block or delete cookies, although some functions may be affected.

We do not use the public website to sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. It has no Leaser advertising pixel. Cookie controls and browser preferences do not necessarily prevent basic server logs. You can contact us about a privacy preference or browser signal using the methods below.

05

How information is used

For the website activities covered by this notice, information is used to:

  • Respond to inquiries, arrange demonstrations, and communicate about your request.
  • Deliver requested research or Learn updates and manage subscription preferences.
  • Evaluate job applications and communicate with candidates about the relevant hiring process.
  • Operate, maintain, troubleshoot, and protect the website and its forms.
  • Understand website performance and improve content, where the enabled tools and your applicable choices permit it.
  • Maintain appropriate business records, comply with legal obligations, and address misuse or legal claims.

Providing an inquiry or job application does not itself subscribe you to a marketing list. If you subscribe to marketing updates, you can use the unsubscribe instructions in those messages or contact us. Necessary responses to your inquiry or application may continue.

06

When information is disclosed

Providers supporting the activity
Information may be disclosed to providers performing authorized functions such as website hosting, security, communications, or application administration, limited to what their work requires and subject to applicable contractual obligations.
Legal and protective purposes
Information may be disclosed when required by law or when reasonably necessary to address fraud, security threats, legal claims, or the rights and safety of people and the business.
Business changes
Information may be considered or transferred in connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, or sale of business assets, subject to appropriate confidentiality protections and applicable law.
At your direction
Information may be disclosed when you direct us to do so or give the necessary permission.
07

Job applicant information

Application information is used to assess your suitability for the role, communicate with you, and administer the recruiting process. Access is intended for people and providers involved in that work.

A request to retain your application for future opportunities should be explained separately, with any choice required by applicable law. Background checks, accommodation requests, or other additional collection may require separate notices and authorizations.

Submitting an application does not create an employment relationship or guarantee an interview or offer.

08

Retention and protection

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for the reason it was collected, considering the status of an inquiry, subscription, or application; applicable recordkeeping duties; security needs; and any legal hold. Different categories may have different retention periods.

Leaser’s data-protection approach calls for safeguards appropriate to the information, including authorized access and secure handling. No transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

09

Your choices and privacy rights

You can choose whether to submit optional information and can stop marketing updates through the unsubscribe instructions. We may need certain contact details to respond to an inquiry or consider an application.

Depending on where you live and which laws apply, you may have rights to access or obtain a copy of your information, correct it, request deletion, opt out of certain disclosures or targeted advertising, limit certain sensitive-information uses, or appeal a decision. These rights can be subject to exceptions.

Where applicable, California rights include knowing, correcting, and deleting personal information; opting out of sale or sharing; limiting certain uses of sensitive information; and receiving equal treatment for exercising those rights.

10

How to make a privacy request

To ask about your information or exercise an applicable right, email leasing@leaserai.com with the subject “Privacy request,” use the contact form and begin your message with “Privacy request,” or call 330.842.7443. Describe your request and provide enough information for us to locate the relevant records. Please do not send sensitive identity documents through the form.

For requests that require identity verification, we may request proportionate information to protect your records. An authorized agent may need to demonstrate permission. Opt-out requests should not be subjected to unnecessary identity verification.

We handle requests within applicable legal timelines. If we cannot fulfill a request, we will explain the reason and any available appeal process. To appeal a decision, use the same email address, contact form, or phone number and identify your message as a privacy appeal.

11

Other sites, locations, and children

Third-party websites reached through links have their own privacy practices. Review those notices before providing information to them.

Leaser’s website is intended for business audiences and job seekers, not children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the website, contact us so the matter can be reviewed.

12

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as website practices or requirements change. The current version will identify its effective date. Material changes will receive additional notice or consent where required.

A revised notice does not, by itself, authorize a new use of information where additional permission is required.
