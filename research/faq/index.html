01 / What Leaser does

Start here.

What is Leaser?

Leaser is an AI-native decision intelligence platform that understands the whole asset and coordinates action across every touchpoint—from first impression to signed lease—to improve NOI and increase enterprise asset value.

It continuously learns from market conditions, demand, digital experience, engagement, prospect behavior, availability, and property economics. Specialized agents evaluate the options, determine the next move, and put approved decisions into action. Every result makes the system smarter about what to do next.

Does Leaser replace our agency and software tools?

Yes. Leaser replaces the outdated agency model and consolidates your software stack.

Demand generation, media execution, prospect intelligence, conversion, reporting, and performance optimization work together in one AI-native platform. Your business gains a system that understands the asset, takes action, and measures the economic impact—with fewer vendors, overlapping subscriptions, and handoffs.

Your team can run Leaser in-house, or work with a supporting team of Leaser experts to guide decisions, execute the plan, and improve performance.

How does Leaser reduce media spend and ILS dependency?

Leaser’s AI-native system uses multiple specialized agents and continuous measurement to reduce media spend by 40% or more.

It evaluates how much additional demand the asset needs, which sources contribute to signed leases, where prospects are being lost, and where spending has stopped earning its place. That intelligence drives budget allocation, channel selection, creative, and conversion improvements.

Leaser also develops demand beyond paid listings, including through the Leaser Demand Network, giving properties more ways to reach renters and less reliance on expensive ILS placements.

What is the Leaser Demand Network?

The Leaser Demand Network is Leaser’s exclusive ad network. It combines enhanced geofencing, proprietary audience signals, and Leaser’s decision intelligence to reach relevant renters beyond the channels you already use.

Leaser aligns that reach with your availability, leasing priorities, and acquisition economics, then measures performance through to signed leases. That gives properties more ways to generate demand, improve media efficiency, and reduce dependence on expensive ILS placements.

How does Leaser turn more prospects into signed leases?

Leaser understands the decision behind the lease: what the prospect wants, what is holding them back, and what will help them move forward.

That intelligence shapes the conversation, the homes presented, the timing of follow-up, and the next action. Market context and competitive alternatives inform the response. When another home within an authorized portfolio is a better fit, Leaser can keep that opportunity within the portfolio.

What Leaser learns from those conversations also improves demand generation, messaging, and the property experience—so each prospect interaction strengthens the next opportunity to win a lease.

How does Leaser improve performance across the whole asset?

Leaser evaluates each move in the context of the whole property and its economics.

Market pressure, availability, demand, prospect behavior, pricing, concessions, and portfolio conditions inform what happens next. The system compares alternatives, simulates potential outcomes, and coordinates action where it will have the greatest impact.

It then measures what changed and carries that learning into the next move. The objective stays consistent: capture more income, eliminate waste, strengthen NOI, and increase enterprise asset value.

Show the reasoning behind decisions and the performance that followed, so your team walks into every meeting ready to take the credit.

03 / How your team works with Leaser

Your team stays in control.

Can our team run Leaser,
or can Leaser run it for us?

Both. Your team can use Leaser directly, or Leaser Managed can operate it for you. Both modes include intelligence and execution across demand, including search, paid media, and the Leaser Demand Network, within your agreed scope.

How much control do we
keep over budgets and
actions?

Your team sets priorities, budgets, and approval boundaries. Leaser evaluates options and coordinates action within that authority, bringing decisions that need your approval back to you.

How does Leaser work
with our existing systems?

We review your current setup to identify what Leaser replaces, what stays, and what needs to connect. The goal is fewer overlapping tools and handoffs, with the information needed to make and execute better decisions.

Can Leaser work across
an entire portfolio?

Yes. Leaser considers each asset’s conditions alongside the wider portfolio. Teams can compare performance, prioritize opportunities, and coordinate demand and prospect opportunities across authorized properties while keeping individual asset goals in view.
