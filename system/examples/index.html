Is the offer worth the faster move-in?

Revenue decision queue
DECISIONCONTEXTSTATUS
Offer a $500 credit on six homes+$2,880 netRecommended
Wait without a concession$0 baselineCompare
Offer one month free−$6,720 netLower value

Recover more rent than the offer costs.

Situation

Six homes rent for $2,100 per month. The model assumes an offer advances move-in by 14 days.

Evidence

Six homes × 14 days × $70/day = $5,880 in recovered rent before the cost of the offer.

Decision

Use a $500 credit on each of the six homes. Total credits cost $3,000.

Action

Prepare the unit-specific offer with consistent terms and eligibility; route it for approval.

AUTHORITYApproval required · Revenue lead

MODELED NET RENTAL INCOME GAIN
+$2,880

$5,880 recovered rent less $3,000 in credits.

84 days

total vacancy days avoided across six homes

Uses a 30-day month and a 14-day acceleration assumption. Other costs are held equal. This one-time gain is not annualized.
