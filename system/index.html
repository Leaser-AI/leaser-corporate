The renter and revenue journey

Every touchpoint belongs to one economic story.

  1. First impressionEnter the consideration set.

    Understand how a renter first encounters a property and which demand source created real awareness.

  2. DemandBuild qualified interest.

    Allocate reach toward the properties and inventory where another renter can change the outcome.

  3. Renter decisionHelp intent progress.

    Connect prospect signals, availability, follow-up, and the operating conditions shaping the choice.

  4. LeaseResolve activity into revenue.

    Carry identity and evidence through application, approval, and signed-lease truth.

  5. Total revenueReturn the economic result.

    Measure what the intervention contributed and make the next portfolio decision stronger.

Portfolio Demand

Put demand where it can create the most value.

Portfolio Demand treats demand as an allocatable asset rather than a property-by-property volume target. It helps determine where demand is truly constrained, which inventory matters, and what the next unit of qualified interest may be worth.
